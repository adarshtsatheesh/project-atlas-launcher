# 🚀 About

**Atlas Launcher** is a modern Android launcher built completely from scratch using **Kotlin** and the latest Android technologies.

Designed with a **glassmorphic**, **minimal**, and **premium** aesthetic, Atlas combines the elegance of iOS, the smoothness of Pixel Launcher, the flexibility of ColorOS, and the intelligence of Material You—while maintaining its own unique identity.

Every interaction is crafted to feel fluid, responsive, and beautiful.

# 🎨 Premium Customization Studio

Atlas introduces a completely redesigned customization experience.

Instead of navigating dozens of settings pages, every change happens inside a **live preview** of your actual home screen.

Changes are applied instantly.

No reloads.

No unnecessary menus.

Just tap, preview and enjoy.
